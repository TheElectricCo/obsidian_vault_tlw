Ephixis, the bow whose ar­ rows herald the turning of the seasons. On a hit, the spear deals damage as normal, and the creature is blinded until the start of its next turn. This property of the spear can't be used again until the next dawn. Spe/Js. The spear regains ld6+4 expended charges daily at dawn. Destroying the Spear. If taken to Erebos's palace in Tizerus, and used to sacrifice a champion of Heliod to Erebos, Khrusor is either destroyed or fundamentally twisted to Erebos's service.
