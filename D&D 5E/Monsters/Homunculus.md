# Homunculus

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Tiny construct, neutral*

## Overview

Homunculus is a tiny construct with challenge rating 0 (10 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 0 | 10 | 13 | 5 | +2 | construct |

- **Armor Class:** 13 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 5 (2d4)
- **Speed:** 20 ft., fly 40 ft.
- **Damage Immunities:** poison
- **Condition Immunities:** charmed, poisoned
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** understands the languages of its creator but can’t speak
- **Initiative:** +2 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +2).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 (-3) | 15 (+2) | 11 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 7 (-2) |

## Traits

**Telepathic Bond.** While the homunculus is on the same plane of existence as its master, it can magically convey what it senses to its master, and the two can communicate telepathically.

## Actions

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 1 piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned for 1 minute. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more, the target is instead poisoned for 5 (1d10) minutes and unconscious while poisoned in this way.
