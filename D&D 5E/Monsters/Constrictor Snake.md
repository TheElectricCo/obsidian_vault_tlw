# Constrictor Snake

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Large beast, unaligned*

## Overview

Constrictor Snake is a large beast with challenge rating 1/4 (50 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1/4 | 50 | 12 | 13 | +2 | beast |

- **Armor Class:** 12
- **Hit Points:** 13 (2d10 + 2)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., swim 30 ft.
- **Senses:** blindsight 10 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** —
- **Initiative:** +2 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +2).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 1 (-5) | 10 (+0) | 3 (-4) |

## Actions

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.

**Constrict.** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) bludgeoning damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 14). Until this grapple ends, the creature is restrained, and the snake can’t constrict another target.
