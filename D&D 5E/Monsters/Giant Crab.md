# Giant Crab

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Medium beast, unaligned*

## Overview

Giant Crab is a medium beast with challenge rating 1/8 (25 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1/8 | 25 | 15 | 13 | +2 | beast |

- **Armor Class:** 15 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 13 (3d8)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., swim 30 ft.
- **Skills:** Stealth +4
- **Senses:** blindsight 30 ft., passive Perception 9
- **Languages:** —
- **Initiative:** +2 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +2).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 13 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 11 (+0) | 1 (-5) | 9 (-1) | 3 (-4) |

## Traits

**Amphibious.** The crab can breathe air and water.

## Actions

**Claw.** Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 4 (1d6 + 1) bludgeoning damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 11). The crab has two claws, each of which can grapple only one target.
