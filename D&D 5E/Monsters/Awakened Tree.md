# Awakened Tree

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Huge plant, unaligned*

## Overview

Awakened Tree is a huge plant with challenge rating 2 (450 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 2 | 450 | 13 | 59 | -2 | plant |

- **Armor Class:** 13 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 59 (7d12 + 14)
- **Speed:** 20 ft.
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** fire
- **Damage Resistances:** bludgeoning, piercing
- **Senses:** passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** one language known by its creator
- **Initiative:** -2 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 -2).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 19 (+4) | 6 (-2) | 15 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 7 (-2) |

## Traits

**False Appearance.** While the tree remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a normal tree.

## Actions

**Slam.** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (3d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage.
