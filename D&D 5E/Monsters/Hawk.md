# Hawk

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Tiny beast, unaligned*

## Overview

Hawk is a tiny beast with challenge rating 0 (10 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 0 | 10 | 13 | 1 | +3 | beast |

- **Armor Class:** 13
- **Hit Points:** 1 (1d4 − 1)
- **Speed:** 10 ft., fly 60 ft.
- **Skills:** Perception +4
- **Senses:** passive Perception 14
- **Languages:** —
- **Initiative:** +3 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +3).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 (-3) | 16 (+3) | 8 (-1) | 2 (-4) | 14 (+2) | 6 (-2) |

## Traits

**Keen Sight.** The hawk has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.

## Actions

**Talons.** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1 slashing damage.
