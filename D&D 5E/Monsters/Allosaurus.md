# Allosaurus

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Large beast, unaligned*

## Overview

A fast predatory dinosaur. A charging claw strike can topple its prey and enable a bonus-action bite. Its bite is more damaging than its claws.
