# Giant Elk

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Huge beast, unaligned*

## Overview

Giant Elk is a huge beast with challenge rating 2 (450 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 2 | 450 | 14 | 42 | +3 | beast |

- **Armor Class:** 14 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 42 (5d12 + 10)
- **Speed:** 60 ft.
- **Skills:** Perception +4
- **Senses:** passive Perception 14
- **Languages:** Giant Elk, understands Common, Elvish, and Sylvan but can’t speak them
- **Initiative:** +3 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +3).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 19 (+4) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 7 (-2) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) |

## Traits

**Charge.** If the elk moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a ram attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 14 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

## Actions

**Ram.** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage.

**Hooves.** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one prone creature. Hit: 22 (4d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage.
