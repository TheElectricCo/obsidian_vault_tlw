# Planetar

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Large celestial, lawful good*

## Overview

Planetar is a large celestial with challenge rating 16 (15,000 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 16 | 15,000 | 19 | 200 | +5 | celestial |

- **Armor Class:** 19 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 200 (16d10 + 112)
- **Speed:** 40 ft., fly 120 ft.
- **Saving Throws:** Con +12, Wis +11, Cha +12
- **Skills:** Perception +11
- **Damage Resistances:** radiant; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Condition Immunities:** charmed, exhaustion, frightened
- **Senses:** truesight 120 ft., passive Perception 21
- **Languages:** all, telepathy 120 ft.
- **Initiative:** +5 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +5).

## Traits

**Angelic Weapons.** The planetar’s weapon attacks are magical. When the planetar hits with any weapon, the weapon deals an extra 5d8 radiant damage (included in the attack).

**Divine Awareness.** The planetar knows if it hears a lie.

**Innate Spellcasting.** The planetar’s spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 20). The planetar can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:

At will: detect evil and good, invisibility (self only)

3/day each: blade barrier, dispel evil and good, flame strike, raise dead

1/day each: commune, control weather, insect plague

**Magic Resistance.** The planetar has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** The planetar makes two melee attacks.

**Greatsword.** Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (4d6 + 7) slashing damage plus 22 (5d8) radiant damage.

**Healing Touch (4/Day).** The planetar touches another creature. The target magically regains 30 (6d8 + 3) hit points and is freed from any curse, disease, poison, blindness, or deafness.
