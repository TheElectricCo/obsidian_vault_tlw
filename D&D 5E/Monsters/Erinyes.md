# Erinyes

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Medium fiend (devil), lawful evil*

## Overview

Erinyes is a medium fiend (devil) with challenge rating 12 (8,400 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 12 | 8,400 | 18 | 153 | +3 | fiend (devil) |

- **Armor Class:** 18 (plate)
- **Hit Points:** 153 (18d8 + 72)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., fly 60 ft.
- **Saving Throws:** Dex +7, Con +8, Wis +6, Cha +8
- **Damage Resistances:** cold; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks that aren’t silvered
- **Damage Immunities:** fire, poison
- **Condition Immunities:** poisoned
- **Senses:** truesight 120 ft., passive Perception 12
- **Languages:** Infernal, telepathy 120 ft.
- **Initiative:** +3 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +3).

## Traits

**Hellish Weapons.** The erinyes’s weapon attacks are magical and deal an extra 13 (3d8) poison damage on a hit (included in the attacks).

**Magic Resistance.** The erinyes has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** The erinyes makes three attacks.

**Longsword.** Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing damage, or 9 (1d10 + 4) slashing damage if used with two hands, plus 13 (3d8) poison damage.

**Longbow.** Ranged Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage plus 13 (3d8) poison damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned. The poison lasts until it is removed by the lesser restoration spell or similar magic.

## Reactions

**Parry.** The erinyes adds 4 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. To do so, the erinyes must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.
