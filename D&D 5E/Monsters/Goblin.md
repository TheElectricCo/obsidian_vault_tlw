# Goblin

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Small humanoid (goblinoid), neutral evil*

## Overview

Goblin is a small humanoid (goblinoid) with challenge rating 1/4 (50 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1/4 | 50 | 15 | 7 | +2 | humanoid (goblinoid) |

- **Armor Class:** 15 (leather armor, shield)
- **Hit Points:** 7 (2d6)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Skills:** Stealth +6
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 9
- **Languages:** Common, Goblin
- **Initiative:** +2 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +2).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8 (-1) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 8 (-1) | 8 (-1) |

## Traits

**Nimble Escape.** The goblin can take the Disengage or Hide action as a bonus action on each of its turns.

## Actions

**Scimitar.** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing damage.

**Shortbow.** Ranged Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.
