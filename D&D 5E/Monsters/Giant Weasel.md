# Giant Weasel

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Medium beast, unaligned*

## Overview

Giant Weasel is a medium beast with challenge rating 1/8 (25 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1/8 | 25 | 13 | 9 | +3 | beast |

- **Armor Class:** 13
- **Hit Points:** 9 (2d8)
- **Speed:** 40 ft.
- **Skills:** Perception +3, Stealth +5
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13
- **Languages:** —
- **Initiative:** +3 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +3).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 11 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) | 4 (-3) | 12 (+1) | 5 (-3) |

## Traits

**Keen Hearing and Smell.** The weasel has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.

## Actions

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage.
