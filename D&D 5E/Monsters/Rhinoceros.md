# Rhinoceros

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Large beast, unaligned*

## Overview

Rhinoceros is a large beast with challenge rating 2 (450 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 2 | 450 | 11 | 45 | -1 | beast |

- **Armor Class:** 11 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 45 (6d10 + 12)
- **Speed:** 40 ft.
- **Senses:** passive Perception 11
- **Languages:** —
- **Initiative:** -1 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 -1).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 21 (+5) | 8 (-1) | 15 (+2) | 2 (-4) | 12 (+1) | 6 (-2) |

## Traits

**Charge.** If the rhinoceros moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a gore attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 9 (2d8) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

## Actions

**Gore.** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage.
