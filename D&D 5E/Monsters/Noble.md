# Noble

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Medium humanoid (any race), any alignment*

## Overview

Noble is a medium humanoid (any race) with challenge rating 1/8 (25 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1/8 | 25 | 15 | 9 | +1 | humanoid (any race) |

- **Armor Class:** 15 (breastplate)
- **Hit Points:** 9 (2d8)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Skills:** Deception +5, Insight +4, Persuasion +5
- **Senses:** passive Perception 12
- **Languages:** any two languages
- **Initiative:** +1 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +1).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 11 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 11 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) |

## Actions

**Rapier.** Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d8 + 1) piercing damage.

## Reactions

**Parry.** The noble adds 2 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. To do so, the noble must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.
