# Giant Eagle

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Large beast, neutral good*

## Overview

Giant Eagle is a large beast with challenge rating 1 (200 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 200 | 13 | 26 | +3 | beast |

- **Armor Class:** 13
- **Hit Points:** 26 (4d10 + 4)
- **Speed:** 10 ft., fly 80 ft.
- **Skills:** Perception +4
- **Senses:** passive Perception 14
- **Languages:** Giant Eagle, understands Common and Auran but can’t speak them
- **Initiative:** +3 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +3).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 (+3) | 17 (+3) | 13 (+1) | 8 (-1) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) |

## Traits

**Keen Sight.** The eagle has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** The eagle makes two attacks: one with its beak and one with its talons.

**Beak.** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.

**Talons.** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) slashing damage.
