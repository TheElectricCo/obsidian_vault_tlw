# Grimlock

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Medium humanoid (grimlock), neutral evil*

## Overview

Grimlock is a medium humanoid (grimlock) with challenge rating 1/4 (50 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1/4 | 50 | 11 | 11 | +1 | humanoid (grimlock) |

- **Armor Class:** 11
- **Hit Points:** 11 (2d8 + 2)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Skills:** Athletics +5, Perception +3, Stealth +3
- **Condition Immunities:** blinded
- **Senses:** blindsight 30 ft. or 10 ft. while deafened (blind beyond this radius), passive Perception 13
- **Languages:** Undercommon
- **Initiative:** +1 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +1).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 12 (+1) | 9 (-1) | 8 (-1) | 6 (-2) |

## Traits

**Blind Senses.** The grimlock can’t use its blindsight while deafened and unable to smell.

**Keen Hearing and Smell.** The grimlock has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.

**Stone Camouflage.** The grimlock has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide in rocky terrain.

## Actions

**Spiked Bone Club.** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d4 + 3) bludgeoning damage plus 2 (1d4) piercing damage.
