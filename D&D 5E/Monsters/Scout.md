# Scout

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Medium humanoid (any race), any alignment*

## Overview

Scout is a medium humanoid (any race) with challenge rating 1/2 (100 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1/2 | 100 | 13 | 16 | +2 | humanoid (any race) |

- **Armor Class:** 13 (leather armor)
- **Hit Points:** 16 (3d8 + 3)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Skills:** Nature +4, Perception +5, Stealth +6, Survival +5
- **Senses:** passive Perception 15
- **Languages:** any one language (usually Common)
- **Initiative:** +2 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +2).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 11 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 11 (+0) | 13 (+1) | 11 (+0) |

## Traits

**Keen Hearing and Sight.** The scout has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or sight.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** The scout makes two melee attacks or two ranged attacks.

**Shortsword.** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.

**Longbow.** Ranged Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, ranged 150/600 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage.
