# Giant Hyena

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Large beast, unaligned*

## Overview

Giant Hyena is a large beast with challenge rating 1 (200 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 200 | 12 | 45 | +2 | beast |

- **Armor Class:** 12
- **Hit Points:** 45 (6d10 + 12)
- **Speed:** 50 ft.
- **Skills:** Perception +3
- **Senses:** passive Perception 13
- **Languages:** —
- **Initiative:** +2 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +2).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 2 (-4) | 12 (+1) | 7 (-2) |

## Traits

**Rampage.** When the hyena reduces a creature to 0 hit points with a melee attack on its turn, the hyena can take a bonus action to move up to half its speed and make a bite attack.

## Actions

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) piercing damage.
