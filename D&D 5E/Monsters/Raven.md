# Raven

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Tiny beast, unaligned*

## Overview

Raven is a tiny beast with challenge rating 0 (10 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 0 | 10 | 12 | 1 | +2 | beast |

- **Armor Class:** 12
- **Hit Points:** 1 (1d4 − 1)
- **Speed:** 10 ft., fly 50 ft.
- **Skills:** Perception +3
- **Senses:** passive Perception 13
- **Languages:** —
- **Initiative:** +2 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +2).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 (-4) | 14 (+2) | 8 (-1) | 2 (-4) | 12 (+1) | 6 (-2) |

## Traits

**Mimicry.** The raven can mimic simple sounds it has heard, such as a person whispering, a baby crying, or an animal chittering. A creature that hears the sounds can tell they are imitations with a successful DC 10 Wisdom (Insight) check.

## Actions

**Beak.** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1 piercing damage.
