# Giant Wasp

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Medium beast, unaligned*

## Overview

Giant Wasp is a medium beast with challenge rating 1/2 (100 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1/2 | 100 | 12 | 13 | +2 | beast |

- **Armor Class:** 12
- **Hit Points:** 13 (3d8)
- **Speed:** 10 ft., fly 50 ft.
- **Senses:** passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** —
- **Initiative:** +2 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +2).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 1 (-5) | 10 (+0) | 3 (-4) |

## Actions

**Sting.** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage, and the target must make a DC 11 Constitution saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. If the poison damage reduces the target to 0 hit points, the target is stable but poisoned for 1 hour, even after regaining hit points, and is paralyzed while poisoned in this way.
