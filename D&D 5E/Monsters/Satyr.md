# Satyr

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Medium fey, chaotic neutral*

## Overview

Satyr is a medium fey with challenge rating 1/2 (100 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1/2 | 100 | 14 | 31 | +3 | fey |

- **Armor Class:** 14 (leather armor)
- **Hit Points:** 31 (7d8)
- **Speed:** 40 ft.
- **Skills:** Perception +2, Performance +6, Stealth +5
- **Senses:** passive Perception 12
- **Languages:** Common, Elvish, Sylvan
- **Initiative:** +3 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +3).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 11 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) |

## Traits

**Magic Resistance.** The satyr has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

## Actions

**Ram.** Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (2d4 + 1) bludgeoning damage.

**Shortsword.** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.

**Shortbow.** Ranged Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.
