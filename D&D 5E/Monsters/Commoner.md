# Commoner

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Medium humanoid (any race), any alignment*

## Overview

Commoner is a medium humanoid (any race) with challenge rating 0 (10 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 0 | 10 | 10 | 4 | +0 | humanoid (any race) |

- **Armor Class:** 10
- **Hit Points:** 4 (1d8)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Senses:** passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** any one language (usually Common)
- **Initiative:** +0 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +0).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) |

## Actions

**Club.** Melee Weapon Attack: +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 2 (1d4) bludgeoning damage.
