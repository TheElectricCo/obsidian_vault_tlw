# Thug

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Medium humanoid (any race), any non-good alignment*

## Overview

Thug is a medium humanoid (any race) with challenge rating 1/2 (100 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1/2 | 100 | 11 | 32 | +0 | humanoid (any race) |

- **Armor Class:** 11 (leather armor)
- **Hit Points:** 32 (5d8 + 10)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Skills:** Intimidation +2
- **Senses:** passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** any one language (usually Common)
- **Initiative:** +0 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +0).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 (+2) | 11 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 11 (+0) |

## Traits

**Pack Tactics.** The thug has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the thug’s allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn’t incapacitated.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** The thug makes two melee attacks.

**Mace.** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage.

**Heavy Crossbow.** Ranged Weapon Attack: +2 to hit, range 100/400 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d10) piercing damage.
