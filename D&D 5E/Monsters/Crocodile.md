# Crocodile

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Large beast, unaligned*

## Overview

Crocodile is a large beast with challenge rating 1/2 (100 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1/2 | 100 | 12 | 19 | +0 | beast |

- **Armor Class:** 12 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 19 (3d10 + 3)
- **Speed:** 20 ft., swim 30 ft.
- **Skills:** Stealth +2
- **Senses:** passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** —
- **Initiative:** +0 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +0).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 13 (+1) | 2 (-4) | 10 (+0) | 5 (-3) |

## Traits

**Hold Breath.** The crocodile can hold its breath for 15 minutes.

## Actions

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 7 (1d10 + 2) piercing damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 12). Until this grapple ends, the target is restrained, and the crocodile can’t bite another target.
