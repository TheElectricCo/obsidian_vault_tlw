# Pteranodon

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Medium beast, unaligned*

## Overview

A flying reptile that attacks with its bite. Its flyby ability lets it leave an enemy’s reach by flight without provoking an opportunity attack.
