# Ape

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Medium beast, unaligned*

## Overview

Ape is a medium beast with challenge rating 1/2 (100 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1/2 | 100 | 12 | 19 | +2 | beast |

- **Armor Class:** 12
- **Hit Points:** 19 (3d8 + 6)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., climb 30 ft.
- **Skills:** Athletics +5, Perception +3
- **Senses:** passive Perception 13
- **Languages:** —
- **Initiative:** +2 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +2).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 6 (-2) | 12 (+1) | 7 (-2) |

## Actions

**Multiattack.** The ape makes two fist attacks.

**Fist.** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage.

**Rock.** Ranged Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, range 25/50 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage.
