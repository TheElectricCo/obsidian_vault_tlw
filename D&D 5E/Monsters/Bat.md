# Bat

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Tiny beast, unaligned*

## Overview

Bat is a tiny beast with challenge rating 0 (10 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 0 | 10 | 12 | 1 | +2 | beast |

- **Armor Class:** 12
- **Hit Points:** 1 (1d4 − 1)
- **Speed:** 5 ft., fly 30 ft.
- **Senses:** blindsight 60 ft., passive Perception 11
- **Languages:** —
- **Initiative:** +2 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +2).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 (-4) | 15 (+2) | 8 (-1) | 2 (-4) | 12 (+1) | 4 (-3) |

## Traits

**Echolocation.** The bat can’t use its blindsight while deafened.

**Keen Hearing.** The bat has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing.

## Actions

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +0 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 1 piercing damage.
