# Scorpion

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Tiny beast, unaligned*

## Overview

Scorpion is a tiny beast with challenge rating 0 (10 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 0 | 10 | 11 | 1 | +0 | beast |

- **Armor Class:** 11 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 1 (1d4 − 1)
- **Speed:** 10 ft.
- **Senses:** blindsight 10 ft., passive Perception 9
- **Languages:** —
- **Initiative:** +0 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +0).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 (-4) | 11 (+0) | 8 (-1) | 1 (-5) | 8 (-1) | 2 (-4) |

## Actions

**Sting.** Melee Weapon Attack: +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 1 piercing damage, and the target must make a DC 9 Constitution saving throw, taking 4 (1d8) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
