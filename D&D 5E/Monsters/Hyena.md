# Hyena

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Medium beast, unaligned*

## Overview

Hyena is a medium beast with challenge rating 0 (10 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 0 | 10 | 11 | 5 | +1 | beast |

- **Armor Class:** 11
- **Hit Points:** 5 (1d8 + 1)
- **Speed:** 50 ft.
- **Skills:** Perception +3
- **Senses:** passive Perception 13
- **Languages:** —
- **Initiative:** +1 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +1).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 11 (+0) | 13 (+1) | 12 (+1) | 2 (-4) | 12 (+1) | 5 (-3) |

## Traits

**Pack Tactics.** The hyena has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the hyena’s allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn’t incapacitated.

## Actions

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 3 (1d6) piercing damage.
