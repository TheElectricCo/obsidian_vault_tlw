# Ghoul

[[D&D 5E/Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Medium undead, chaotic evil*

## Overview

Ghoul is a medium undead with challenge rating 1 (200 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 200 | 12 | 22 | +2 | undead |

- **Armor Class:** 12
- **Hit Points:** 22 (5d8)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Damage Immunities:** poison
- **Condition Immunities:** charmed, exhaustion, poisoned
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common
- **Initiative:** +2 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +2).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 13 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 7 (-2) | 10 (+0) | 6 (-2) |

## Actions

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 9 (2d6 + 2) piercing damage.

**Claws.** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (2d4 + 2) slashing damage. If the target is a creature other than an elf or undead, it must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or be paralyzed for 1 minute. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
