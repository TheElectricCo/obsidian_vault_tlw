# Potion of Gaseous Form

[[D&D 5E/Magic Items Glossary|← Magic Items Glossary]]

*Potion, rare*

## Item Details

- **Type:** Potion
- **Rarity:** rare
- **Attunement:** No

## Description

When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the [[D&D 5E/Spells/Gaseous Form|gaseous form]] spell for 1 hour (no concentration required) or until you end the effect as a bonus action. This potion’s container seems to hold fog that moves and pours like water.
