# Potion of Diminution

[[D&D 5E/Magic Items Glossary|← Magic Items Glossary]]

*Potion, rare*

## Item Details

- **Type:** Potion
- **Rarity:** rare
- **Attunement:** No

## Description

When you drink this potion, you gain the “reduce” effect of the [[D&D 5E/Spells/Enlarge - Reduce|enlarge/reduce]] spell for 1d4 hours (no concentration required). The red in the potion’s liquid continuously contracts to a tiny bead and then expands to color the clear liquid around it. Shaking the bottle fails to interrupt this process.
