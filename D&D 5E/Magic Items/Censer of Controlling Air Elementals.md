# Censer of Controlling Air Elementals

[[D&D 5E/Magic Items Glossary|← Magic Items Glossary]]

*Wondrous item, rare*

## Item Details

- **Type:** Wondrous item
- **Rarity:** rare
- **Attunement:** No

## Description

While incense is burning in this censer, you can use an action to speak the censer’s command word and summon an air elemental, as if you had cast the [[D&D 5E/Spells/Conjure Elemental|conjure elemental]] spell. The censer can’t be used this way again until the next dawn.

This 6-inch-wide, 1-foot-high vessel resembles a chalice with a decorated lid. It weighs 1 pound.
