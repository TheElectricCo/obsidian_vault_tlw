# Ring of Animal Influence

[[D&D 5E/Magic Items Glossary|← Magic Items Glossary]]

*Ring, rare*

## Item Details

- **Type:** Ring
- **Rarity:** rare
- **Attunement:** No

## Description

This ring has 3 charges, and it regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the ring, you can use an action to expend 1 of its charges to cast one of the following spells:

- [[D&D 5E/Spells/Animal Friendship|Animal friendship]] (save DC 13)
- [[D&D 5E/Spells/Fear|Fear]] (save DC 13), targeting only beasts that have an Intelligence of 3 or lower
- [[D&D 5E/Spells/Speak with Animals|Speak with animals]]
