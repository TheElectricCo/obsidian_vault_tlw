# Potion of Animal Friendship

[[D&D 5E/Magic Items Glossary|← Magic Items Glossary]]

*Potion, uncommon*

## Item Details

- **Type:** Potion
- **Rarity:** uncommon
- **Attunement:** No

## Description

When you drink this potion, you can cast the [[D&D 5E/Spells/Animal Friendship|animal friendship]] spell (save DC 13) for 1 hour at will. Agitating this muddy liquid brings little bits into view: a fish scale, a hummingbird tongue, a cat claw, or a squirrel hair.
