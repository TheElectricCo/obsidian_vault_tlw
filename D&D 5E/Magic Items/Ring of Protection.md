# Ring of Protection

[[D&D 5E/Magic Items Glossary|← Magic Items Glossary]]

*Ring, rare (requires attunement)*

## Item Details

- **Type:** Ring
- **Rarity:** rare
- **Attunement:** Yes

## Description

You gain a +1 bonus to AC and saving throws while wearing this ring.
