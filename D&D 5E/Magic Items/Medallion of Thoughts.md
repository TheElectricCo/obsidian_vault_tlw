# Medallion of Thoughts

[[D&D 5E/Magic Items Glossary|← Magic Items Glossary]]

*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*

## Item Details

- **Type:** Wondrous item
- **Rarity:** uncommon
- **Attunement:** Yes

## Description

The medallion has 3 charges. While wearing it, you can use an action and expend 1 charge to cast the [[D&D 5E/Spells/Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]] spell (save DC 13) from it. The medallion regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.
