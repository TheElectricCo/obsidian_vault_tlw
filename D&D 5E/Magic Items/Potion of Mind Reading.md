# Potion of Mind Reading

[[D&D 5E/Magic Items Glossary|← Magic Items Glossary]]

*Potion, rare*

## Item Details

- **Type:** Potion
- **Rarity:** rare
- **Attunement:** No

## Description

When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the [[D&D 5E/Spells/Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]] spell (save DC 13). The potion’s dense, purple liquid has an ovoid cloud of pink floating in it.
