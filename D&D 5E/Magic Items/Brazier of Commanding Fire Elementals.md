# Brazier of Commanding Fire Elementals

[[D&D 5E/Magic Items Glossary|← Magic Items Glossary]]

*Wondrous item, rare*

## Item Details

- **Type:** Wondrous item
- **Rarity:** rare
- **Attunement:** No

## Description

While a fire burns in this brass brazier, you can use an action to speak the brazier’s command word and summon a fire elemental, as if you had cast the [[D&D 5E/Spells/Conjure Elemental|conjure elemental]] spell. The brazier can’t be used this way again until the next dawn.

The brazier weighs 5 pounds.
