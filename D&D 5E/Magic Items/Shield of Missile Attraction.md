# Shield of Missile Attraction

[[D&D 5E/Magic Items Glossary|← Magic Items Glossary]]

*Armor (shield), rare (requires attunement)*

## Item Details

- **Type:** Armor (shield)
- **Rarity:** rare
- **Attunement:** Yes

## Description

While holding this shield, you have resistance to damage from ranged weapon attacks.

Curse. This shield is cursed. Attuning to it curses you until you are targeted by the [[D&D 5E/Spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]] spell or similar magic. Removing the shield fails to end the curse on you. Whenever a ranged weapon attack is made against a target within 10 feet of you, the curse causes you to become the target instead.
