# Magic Items Glossary - 2014 Rules

An alphabetical reference to **241 magic item entries** from [D&D Beyond’s Basic Rules (2014)](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/magic-items), including the Orb of Dragonkind artifact. Each entry links to its note in `D&D 5E/Magic Items`, and each note links back here.

- **Type:** item category and applicable equipment restrictions.
- **Rarity:** the listed rarity, including different rarities for variants.
- **Attunement:** whether attunement is required and any prerequisites. Additional usage restrictions, curses, charges, and recharge rules appear in each description.
- Variant families such as +1/+2/+3 equipment, potions of healing, and figurines of wondrous power remain grouped as they are in the source; their descriptions and tables distinguish the individual variants.
- 240 entries use material shared with the openly licensed **SRD 5.1**. Potion of Vitality has an original concise mechanics summary and a link to the full source description.
- Keoghtom’s Ointment appears in SRD 5.1 as Restorative Ointment. The Orb of Dragonkind note uses the SRD’s setting-neutral description.
- Prices are not listed because the chapter does not supply fixed purchase prices for these entries.
