# Monsters Glossary - 2014 Rules

An alphabetical reference to **326 monster and NPC stat blocks** from [D&D Beyond’s Basic Rules (2014)](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/monsters), including its A–Z sections and NPC descriptions.

- **CR:** challenge rating. **XP:** experience points per creature.
- **AC:** default armor class; alternate armor and spell effects appear in the individual note.
- **HP:** listed average hit points; the note also gives the hit dice.
- **Initiative:** Dexterity modifier, calculated as ⌊(DEX − 10) / 2⌋. Add this to a d20 roll; it is not an initiative score or a Dexterity saving throw bonus.
- **Type:** creature type and any parenthetical tags; swarm entries retain their swarm classification.
- Each monster name links to its note in `D&D 5E/Monsters`; every note links back here.

Notes for the **317 SRD creatures** include their stat-block traits and actions, using material shared with the openly licensed SRD 5.1. The **9 additional Basic Rules creatures** (Allosaurus, Ankylosaurus, Banshee, Cyclops, Flameskull, Nothic, Pteranodon, Spectator, and Twig Blight) have factual statistics, original short summaries, and source links for their full rules. Narrative lore, optional variants, and separate lair sections are not reproduced.

The Storm Giant note restores its omitted CR and skills and its original spellcasting and recharge rules from SRD 5.1; the current web chapter differs.
