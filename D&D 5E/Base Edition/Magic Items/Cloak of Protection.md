# Cloak of Protection

[[Magic Items Glossary|← Magic Items Glossary]]

*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*

## Item Details

- **Type:** Wondrous item
- **Rarity:** uncommon
- **Attunement:** Yes

## Description

You gain a +1 bonus to AC and saving throws while you wear this cloak.
