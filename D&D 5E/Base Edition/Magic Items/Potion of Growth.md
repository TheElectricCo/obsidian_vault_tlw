# Potion of Growth

[[Magic Items Glossary|← Magic Items Glossary]]

*Potion, uncommon*

## Item Details

- **Type:** Potion
- **Rarity:** uncommon
- **Attunement:** No

## Description

When you drink this potion, you gain the “enlarge” effect of the [[Enlarge - Reduce|enlarge/reduce]] spell for 1d4 hours (no concentration required). The red in the potion’s liquid continuously expands from a tiny bead to color the clear liquid around it and then contracts. Shaking the bottle fails to interrupt this process.
