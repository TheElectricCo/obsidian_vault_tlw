# Ring of Djinni Summoning

[[Magic Items Glossary|← Magic Items Glossary]]

*Ring, legendary (requires attunement)*

## Item Details

- **Type:** Ring
- **Rarity:** legendary
- **Attunement:** Yes

## Description

While wearing this ring, you can speak its command word as an action to summon a particular [[Djinni|djinni]] from the Elemental Plane of Air. The [[Djinni|djinni]] appears in an unoccupied space you choose within 120 feet of you. It remains as long as you concentrate (as if concentrating on a spell), to a maximum of 1 hour, or until it drops to 0 hit points. It then returns to its home plane.

While summoned, the [[Djinni|djinni]] is friendly to you and your companions. It obeys any commands you give it, no matter what language you use. If you fail to command it, the [[Djinni|djinni]] defends itself against attackers but takes no other actions.

After the [[Djinni|djinni]] departs, it can’t be summoned again for 24 hours, and the ring becomes nonmagical if the [[Djinni|djinni]] dies.
