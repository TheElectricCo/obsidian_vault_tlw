# Potion of Vitality

[[Magic Items Glossary|← Magic Items Glossary]]

*Potion, very rare*

## Item Details

- **Type:** Potion
- **Rarity:** very rare
- **Attunement:** No

## Description

Drinking this potion clears exhaustion and cures diseases and poison affecting the drinker. For the following 24 hours, each Hit Die spent restores its maximum possible hit points.
