# Mantle of Spell Resistance

[[Magic Items Glossary|← Magic Items Glossary]]

*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*

## Item Details

- **Type:** Wondrous item
- **Rarity:** rare
- **Attunement:** Yes

## Description

You have advantage on saving throws against spells while you wear this cloak.
