# Ring of Swimming

[[Magic Items Glossary|← Magic Items Glossary]]

*Ring, uncommon*

## Item Details

- **Type:** Ring
- **Rarity:** uncommon
- **Attunement:** No

## Description

You have a swimming speed of 40 feet while wearing this ring.
