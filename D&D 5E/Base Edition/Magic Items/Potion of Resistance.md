# Potion of Resistance

[[Magic Items Glossary|← Magic Items Glossary]]

*Potion, uncommon*

## Item Details

- **Type:** Potion
- **Rarity:** uncommon
- **Attunement:** No

## Description

When you drink this potion, you gain resistance to one type of damage for 1 hour. The DM chooses the type or determines it randomly from the options below.

| d10 | Damage Type | d10 | Damage Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Acid | 6 | Necrotic |
| 2 | Cold | 7 | Poison |
| 3 | Fire | 8 | Psychic |
| 4 | Force | 9 | Radiant |
| 5 | Lightning | 10 | Thunder |
