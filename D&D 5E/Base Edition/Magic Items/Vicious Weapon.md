# Vicious Weapon

[[Magic Items Glossary|← Magic Items Glossary]]

*Weapon (any), rare*

## Item Details

- **Type:** Weapon (any)
- **Rarity:** rare
- **Attunement:** No

## Description

When you roll a 20 on your attack roll with this magic weapon, the target takes an extra 7 damage of the weapon’s type.
