# Orc

[[Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Medium humanoid (orc), chaotic evil*

## Overview

Orc is a medium humanoid (orc) with challenge rating 1/2 (100 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1/2 | 100 | 13 | 15 | +1 | humanoid (orc) |

- **Armor Class:** 13 (hide armor)
- **Hit Points:** 15 (2d8 + 6)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Skills:** Intimidation +2
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common, Orc
- **Initiative:** +1 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +1).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 7 (-2) | 11 (+0) | 10 (+0) |

## Traits

**Aggressive.** As a bonus action, the orc can move up to its speed toward a hostile creature that it can see.

## Actions

**Greataxe.** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (1d12 + 3) slashing damage.

**Javelin.** Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.
