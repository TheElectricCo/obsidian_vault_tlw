*Large fiend (devil), lawful evil*

## Overview

Bone Devil is a large fiend (devil) with challenge rating 9 (5,000 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 9 | 5,000 | 19 | 142 | +3 | fiend (devil) |

- **Armor Class:** 19 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 142 (15d10 + 60)
- **Speed:** 40 ft., fly 40 ft.
- **Saving Throws:** Int +5, Wis +6, Cha +7
- **Skills:** Deception +7, Insight +6
- **Damage Resistances:** cold; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks that aren’t silvered
- **Damage Immunities:** fire, poison
- **Condition Immunities:** poisoned
- **Senses:** darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 12
- **Languages:** Infernal, telepathy 120 ft.
- **Initiative:** +3 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +3).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 18 (+4) | 16 (+3) | 18 (+4) | 13 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) |

## Traits

**Devil’s Sight.** Magical darkness doesn’t impede the devil’s darkvision.

**Magic Resistance.** The devil has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** The devil makes three attacks: two with its claws and one with its sting.

**Claw.** Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing damage.

**Sting.** Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) piercing damage plus 17 (5d6) poison damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or become poisoned for 1 minute. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
