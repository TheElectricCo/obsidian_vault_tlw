# Hippogriff

[[Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Large monstrosity, unaligned*

## Overview

Hippogriff is a large monstrosity with challenge rating 1 (200 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 200 | 11 | 19 | +1 | monstrosity |

- **Armor Class:** 11
- **Hit Points:** 19 (3d10 + 3)
- **Speed:** 40 ft., fly 60 ft.
- **Skills:** Perception +5
- **Senses:** passive Perception 15
- **Languages:** —
- **Initiative:** +1 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +1).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 17 (+3) | 13 (+1) | 13 (+1) | 2 (-4) | 12 (+1) | 8 (-1) |

## Traits

**Keen Sight.** The hippogriff has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** The hippogriff makes two attacks: one with its beak and one with its claws.

**Beak.** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d10 + 3) piercing damage.

**Claws.** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) slashing damage.
