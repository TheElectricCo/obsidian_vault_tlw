# Swarm of Quippers

[[Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Medium swarm of Tiny beasts, unaligned*

## Overview

Swarm of Quippers is a medium swarm of tiny beasts with challenge rating 1 (200 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 200 | 13 | 28 | +3 | swarm of Tiny beasts |

- **Armor Class:** 13
- **Hit Points:** 28 (8d8 − 8)
- **Speed:** 0 ft., swim 40 ft.
- **Damage Resistances:** bludgeoning, piercing, slashing
- **Condition Immunities:** charmed, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, prone, restrained, stunned
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 8
- **Languages:** —
- **Initiative:** +3 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +3).

## Traits

**Blood Frenzy.** The swarm has advantage on melee attack rolls against any creature that doesn’t have all its hit points.

**Swarm.** The swarm can occupy another creature’s space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny quipper. The swarm can’t regain hit points or gain temporary hit points.

**Water Breathing.** The swarm can breathe only underwater.

## Actions

**Bites.** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 0 ft., one creature in the swarm’s space. Hit: 14 (4d6) piercing damage, or 7 (2d6) piercing damage if the swarm has half of its hit points or fewer.
