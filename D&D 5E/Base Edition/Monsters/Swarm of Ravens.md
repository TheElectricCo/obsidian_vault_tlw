# Swarm of Ravens

[[Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Medium swarm of Tiny beasts, unaligned*

## Overview

Swarm of Ravens is a medium swarm of tiny beasts with challenge rating 1/4 (50 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1/4 | 50 | 12 | 24 | +2 | swarm of Tiny beasts |

- **Armor Class:** 12
- **Hit Points:** 24 (7d8 − 7)
- **Speed:** 10 ft., fly 50 ft.
- **Skills:** Perception +5
- **Damage Resistances:** bludgeoning, piercing, slashing
- **Condition Immunities:** charmed, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, prone, restrained, stunned
- **Senses:** passive Perception 15
- **Languages:** —
- **Initiative:** +2 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +2).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6 (-2) | 14 (+2) | 8 (-1) | 3 (-4) | 12 (+1) | 6 (-2) |

## Traits

**Swarm.** The swarm can occupy another creature’s space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny raven. The swarm can’t regain hit points or gain temporary hit points.

## Actions

**Beaks.** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target in the swarm’s space. Hit: 7 (2d6) piercing damage, or 3 (1d6) piercing damage if the swarm has half of its hit points or fewer.
