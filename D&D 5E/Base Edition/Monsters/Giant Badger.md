*Medium beast, unaligned*

## Overview

Giant Badger is a medium beast with challenge rating 1/4 (50 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1/4 | 50 | 10 | 13 | +0 | beast |

- **Armor Class:** 10
- **Hit Points:** 13 (2d8 + 4)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., burrow 10 ft.
- **Senses:** darkvision 30 ft., passive Perception 11
- **Languages:** —
- **Initiative:** +0 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +0).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 13 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 15 (+2) | 2 (-4) | 12 (+1) | 5 (-3) |

## Traits

**Keen Smell.** The badger has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** The badger makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws.

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing damage.

**Claws.** Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (2d4 + 1) slashing damage.
