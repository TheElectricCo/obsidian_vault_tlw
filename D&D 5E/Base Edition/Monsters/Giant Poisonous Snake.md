## Overview

Giant Poisonous Snake is a medium beast with challenge rating 1/4 (50 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1/4 | 50 | 14 | 11 | +4 | beast |

- **Armor Class:** 14
- **Hit Points:** 11 (2d8 + 2)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., swim 30 ft.
- **Skills:** Perception +2
- **Senses:** blindsight 10 ft., passive Perception 12
- **Languages:** —
- **Initiative:** +4 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +4).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 (+0) | 18 (+4) | 13 (+1) | 2 (-4) | 10 (+0) | 3 (-4) |

## Actions

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d4 + 4) piercing damage, and the target must make a DC 11 Constitution saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
