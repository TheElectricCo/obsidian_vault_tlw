# Steam Mephit

[[Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Small elemental, neutral evil*

## Overview

Steam Mephit is a small elemental with challenge rating 1/4 (50 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1/4 | 50 | 10 | 21 | +0 | elemental |

- **Armor Class:** 10
- **Hit Points:** 21 (6d6)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., fly 30 ft.
- **Damage Immunities:** fire, poison
- **Condition Immunities:** poisoned
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Aquan, Ignan
- **Initiative:** +0 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +0).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 (-3) | 11 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 11 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) |

## Traits

**Death Burst.** When the mephit dies, it explodes in a cloud of steam. Each creature within 5 feet of the mephit must succeed on a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw or take 4 (1d8) fire damage.

**Innate Spellcasting (1/Day).** The mephit can innately cast blur, requiring no material components. Its innate spellcasting ability is Charisma.

## Actions

**Claws.** Melee Weapon Attack: +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 2 (1d4) slashing damage plus 2 (1d4) fire damage.

**Steam Breath (Recharge 6).** The mephit exhales a 15-foot cone of scalding steam. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw, taking 4 (1d8) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
