# Invisible Stalker

[[Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Medium elemental, neutral*

## Overview

Invisible Stalker is a medium elemental with challenge rating 6 (2,300 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 6 | 2,300 | 14 | 104 | +4 | elemental |

- **Armor Class:** 14
- **Hit Points:** 104 (16d8 + 32)
- **Speed:** 50 ft., fly 50 ft. (hover)
- **Skills:** Perception +8, Stealth +10
- **Damage Resistances:** bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Immunities:** poison
- **Condition Immunities:** exhaustion, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained, unconscious
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 18
- **Languages:** Auran, understands Common but doesn’t speak it
- **Initiative:** +4 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +4).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 (+3) | 19 (+4) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 15 (+2) | 11 (+0) |

## Traits

**Invisibility.** The stalker is invisible.

**Faultless Tracker.** The stalker is given a quarry by its summoner. The stalker knows the direction and distance to its quarry as long as the two of them are on the same plane of existence. The stalker also knows the location of its summoner.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** The stalker makes two slam attacks.

**Slam.** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage.
