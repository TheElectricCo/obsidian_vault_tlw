## Overview

Giant Goat is a large beast with challenge rating 1/2 (100 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1/2 | 100 | 11 | 19 | +0 | beast |

- **Armor Class:** 11 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 19 (3d10 + 3)
- **Speed:** 40 ft.
- **Senses:** passive Perception 11
- **Languages:** —
- **Initiative:** +0 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +0).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 17 (+3) | 11 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 3 (-4) | 12 (+1) | 6 (-2) |

## Traits

**Charge.** If the goat moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a ram attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 5 (2d4) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

**Sure-Footed.** The goat has advantage on Strength and Dexterity saving throws made against effects that would knock it prone.

## Actions

**Ram.** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (2d4 + 3) bludgeoning damage.
