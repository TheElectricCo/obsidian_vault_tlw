# Hobgoblin

[[Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Medium humanoid (goblinoid), lawful evil*

## Overview

Hobgoblin is a medium humanoid (goblinoid) with challenge rating 1/2 (100 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1/2 | 100 | 18 | 11 | +1 | humanoid (goblinoid) |

- **Armor Class:** 18 (chain mail, shield)
- **Hit Points:** 11 (2d8 + 2)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common, Goblin
- **Initiative:** +1 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +1).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 13 (+1) | 12 (+1) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 9 (-1) |

## Traits

**Martial Advantage.** Once per turn, the hobgoblin can deal an extra 7 (2d6) damage to a creature it hits with a weapon attack if that creature is within 5 feet of an ally of the hobgoblin that isn’t incapacitated.

## Actions

**Longsword.** Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d8 + 1) slashing damage, or 6 (1d10 + 1) slashing damage if used with two hands.

**Longbow.** Ranged Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d8 + 1) piercing damage.
