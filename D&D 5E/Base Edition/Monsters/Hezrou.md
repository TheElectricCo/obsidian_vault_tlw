*Large fiend (demon), chaotic evil*

## Overview

Hezrou is a large fiend (demon) with challenge rating 8 (3,900 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 8 | 3,900 | 16 | 136 | +3 | fiend (demon) |

- **Armor Class:** 16 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 136 (13d10 + 65)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Saving Throws:** Str +7, Con +8, Wis +4
- **Damage Resistances:** cold, fire, lightning; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Immunities:** poison
- **Condition Immunities:** poisoned
- **Senses:** darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 11
- **Languages:** Abyssal, telepathy 120 ft.
- **Initiative:** +3 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +3).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 19 (+4) | 17 (+3) | 20 (+5) | 5 (-3) | 12 (+1) | 13 (+1) |

## Traits

**Magic Resistance.** The hezrou has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Stench.** Any creature that starts its turn within 10 feet of the hezrou must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the start of its next turn. On a successful saving throw, the creature is immune to the hezrou’s stench for 24 hours.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** The hezrou makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its claws.

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d10 + 4) piercing damage.

**Claw.** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage.
