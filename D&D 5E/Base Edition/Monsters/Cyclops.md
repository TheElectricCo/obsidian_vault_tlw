# Cyclops

[[Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Huge giant, chaotic neutral*

## Overview

A towering giant that fights with a greatclub or thrown rocks. It can swing its club twice per action, but its poor depth perception hampers attacks beyond 30 feet.
