# Banshee

[[Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Medium undead, chaotic evil*

## Overview

A spectral undead that senses nearby life and passes through solid matter. Its appearance terrifies witnesses, its touch deals necrotic damage, and its daily wail can drop listeners to zero hit points. Sunlight prevents the wail.

> This note contains a factual reference and a brief ability summary. Use the linked 2014 Basic Rules entry for complete action wording, spell lists, saving throws, and durations.
