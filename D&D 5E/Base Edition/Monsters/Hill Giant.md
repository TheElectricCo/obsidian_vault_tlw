# Hill Giant

[[Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Huge giant, chaotic evil*

## Overview

Hill Giant is a huge giant with challenge rating 5 (1,800 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 5 | 1,800 | 13 | 105 | -1 | giant |

- **Armor Class:** 13 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 105 (10d12 + 40)
- **Speed:** 40 ft.
- **Skills:** Perception +2
- **Senses:** passive Perception 12
- **Languages:** Giant
- **Initiative:** -1 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 -1).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 21 (+5) | 8 (-1) | 19 (+4) | 5 (-3) | 9 (-1) | 6 (-2) |

## Actions

**Multiattack.** The giant makes two greatclub attacks.

**Greatclub.** Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (3d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage.

**Rock.** Ranged Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, range 60/240 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (3d10 + 5) bludgeoning damage.
