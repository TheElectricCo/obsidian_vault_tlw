# Draft Horse

[[Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Large beast, unaligned*

## Overview

Draft Horse is a large beast with challenge rating 1/4 (50 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1/4 | 50 | 10 | 19 | +0 | beast |

- **Armor Class:** 10
- **Hit Points:** 19 (3d10 + 3)
- **Speed:** 40 ft.
- **Senses:** passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** —
- **Initiative:** +0 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +0).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |

**Hooves.** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (2d4 + 4) bludgeoning damage.
