# Storm Giant

[[Monsters Glossary|← Monsters Glossary]]

*Huge giant, chaotic good*

## Overview

> Source correction: the current Basic Rules web chapter omits this creature’s CR and skills and shows different spellcasting and recharge rules. This note uses the original SRD 5.1 stat block (pp. 313–314) for those fields.

Storm Giant is a huge giant with challenge rating 13 (10,000 XP). Its traits and combat options are detailed below.

## Statistics

| CR | XP | AC | HP | Initiative | Type |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 13 | 10,000 | 16 | 230 | +2 | giant |

- **Armor Class:** 16 (scale mail)
- **Hit Points:** 230 (20d12 + 100)
- **Speed:** 50 ft., swim 50 ft.
- **Saving Throws:** Str +14, Con +10, Wis +9, Cha +9
- **Damage Resistances:** cold
- **Damage Immunities:** lightning, thunder
- **Senses:** passive Perception 19
- **Languages:** Common, Giant
- **Skills:** Arcana +8, Athletics +14, History +8, Perception +9
- **Initiative:** +2 (Dexterity modifier; roll 1d20 +2).

## Traits

**Amphibious.** The giant can breathe air and water.

**Innate Spellcasting.** The giant’s innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 17). It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:

- At will: detect magic, feather fall, levitate, light
- 3/day each: control weather, water breathing

## Actions

Multiattack. The giant makes two greatsword attacks.

Greatsword. Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 30 (6d6 + 9) slashing damage.

Rock. Ranged Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, range 60/240 ft., one target. Hit: 35 (4d12 + 9) bludgeoning damage.

**Lightning Strike (Recharge 5–6).** The giant hurls a magical lightning bolt at a point it can see within 500 feet of it. Each creature within 10 feet of that point must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 54 (12d8) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
