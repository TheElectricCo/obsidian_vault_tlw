
## Overview

Altrisos is a city of **Meletis** and is described as the finest example of prosperity in all of Theros. It is the most prosperous of the Meletian settlements and is considered blessed by **Ephara**. The city is fervently loyal to the goddess.

The city’s most famous tale concerns the repulse of a colossal kraken. According to the account, an effigy—or appearance in effigy—of Ephara upon the city walls drove the kraken away, while the goddess raised the walls to protect Altrisos’ inhabitants.

## Bay and Harbor

Altrisos has a broad bay surrounded by piers. The harbor is generally not crowded, but its layout makes it convenient for merchants and travelers. A colossal sculpture of Ephara stands in the centre of the bay to welcome arriving ships.

The northern harbor is chiefly devoted to trade, while the southern harbor is mainly used for fishing. In practice, the two activities regularly overlap. The large agora square, with its stepped stone quay, also serves as a place for ceremonial receptions.
