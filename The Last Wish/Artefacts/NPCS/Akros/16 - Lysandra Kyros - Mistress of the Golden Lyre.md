# Lysandra Kyros — Mistress of the Golden Lyre

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> Lysandra’s name, position as mistress of the Golden Lyre, and invitation to [[Arowel Starweaver]] for the Iroan Games are established in Arowel’s player note and prologue. Her appearance, financing dispute, and working methods are new. The reason behind the invitation remains open beyond its stated artistic purpose.

**Role:** Artistic director and patron of visiting performers  
**Identity:** Human woman, 47; a formidable organiser with a musician’s ear.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 16 — the performance hall directly below the arena (15), near the right edge of the map.
- **Usual meeting spot:** The fountain courtyard before rehearsals; the rear of the performance hall during auditions.

## First impression

A golden lyre clasp gleams against dark blue cloth. She stills a room with two raised fingers, then listens so intently that even a tuning string seems worth hearing.

## Playing Lysandra Kyros

Gracious with nerves, merciless with lazy preparation. Taps rhythms against her wrist. “Let me hear the part you nearly crossed out.”

**What they want:** Present a Games programme worthy of the city while protecting performers from patrons who expect obedience with their applause.

**What they can offer:** Introductions to artists, rehearsal space by arrangement, and access to public song collections. She can support research into old tales without proving an artefact’s existence or whereabouts.

## Secret

A major sponsor is withholding payment unless she removes a lament for unnamed workers from the programme. She has mortgaged her own property to cover the musicians’ first instalment.

## A scene ready to play

A rehearsal breaks down when hired musicians learn that payment may fail. Lysandra asks for mediation, a replacement patron, or help negotiating an honest public programme. If Arowel presents the established invitation, she honours it with a hearing and rehearsal opportunity; the player decides how to respond.

**If left unresolved:** If no solution appears, she reduces the production and pays the least wealthy performers first. Her invitation remains valid, although the event changes.
