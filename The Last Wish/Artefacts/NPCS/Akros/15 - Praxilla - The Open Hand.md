# Praxilla — The Open Hand

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> New campaign NPC; all personal history and hooks below are new material.

**Role:** Arena referee and former wrestling champion  
**Identity:** Human woman, 44; a veteran competitor devoted to fair contests.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 15 — the great arena at the upper right of the map, immediately above the Golden Lyre (16).
- **Usual meeting spot:** The shaded competitors’ entrance, watching the sand through the tunnel mouth.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/15-Arena.png|15 — The Arena]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

Her nose has been broken more than once. She wears a white judging sash over a plain red tunic and carries no weapon. A small clay token clicks between two thick fingers.

## Playing Praxilla

Cheerful until a participant cannot freely consent. Demands plain rules before wagers. “Tell me what counts as winning before someone starts bleeding.”

**What they want:** Keep an exhibition from becoming a staged punishment disguised as sport.

**What they can offer:** Published contest rules, introductions to athletes, and a fair hearing on an entry dispute. She promises neither victory nor immunity from agreed rules.

## Secret

She once accepted a sponsor’s payment to overlook an ineligible competitor. No one was hurt, but the ledger could make her present objections look purchased.

## A scene ready to play

A debtor has been entered in a supposedly voluntary bout, and the sponsor refuses to let him withdraw. Praxilla asks the party to obtain the actual agreement or negotiate his release before the next announcement. A fair substitute contest, debt settlement, or public exposure are all possible.

**If left unresolved:** If the bout is not resolved, she refuses to officiate it and is replaced. There remains time to appeal to other officials or approach the competitor.
