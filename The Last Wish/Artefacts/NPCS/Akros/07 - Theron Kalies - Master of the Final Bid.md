> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> Theron’s name, title, auction house, invitation to [[Darius Voss]], and Lot XVII’s advertised connection to Varyas are established in [[The Last Wish/Sessions/000 - Backstories/Log/Darius]]. **Role:** First auction master of the House of the Golden Spear  
**Identity:** Human man, 50; immaculate broker of reputation and rare objects.

## First impression

A single gold spear pin fastens a dark mantle. His hands are bare and carefully washed; he touches valuable objects only through linen. His smile arrives a heartbeat after his assessment.

## Playing Theron Kalies

Courteous, patient, and punctilious about exact wording. “The catalogue tells you what is offered. Your questions tell me what you are buying.”

**What they want:** Run an impeccable auction without allowing an uncertain provenance to destroy his house’s credibility.

**What they can offer:** Written bidding terms, an appointment to inspect permitted material, and introductions to relevant specialists. Access to restricted items requires his agreement; a catalogue title never proves authenticity.

## Secret

A supporting document in a consignment dossier is a later copy whose original he has not seen. He has postponed deciding whether to disclose that weakness, fearing a rival will make the announcement first.

## A scene ready to play

A viewing appointment for Lot XVII is interrupted by a demand that it be withdrawn. Theron asks the visitors to witness which records actually exist before accusations spread. They may seek an independent appraisal, negotiate inspection conditions, or insist the catalogue distinguish attribution from proof.

**If left unresolved:** If pressed in public, he suspends the disputed viewing and requests a formal review. He will bargain over procedure but will not casually surrender a consignment.
