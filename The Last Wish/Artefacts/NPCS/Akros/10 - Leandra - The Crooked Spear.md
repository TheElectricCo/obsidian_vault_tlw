# Leandra — The Crooked Spear

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> New campaign NPC; all personal history and hooks below are new material.

**Role:** Drill instructor  
**Identity:** Human woman, 39; a veteran with a partly stiff left hand.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 10 — the practice courtyard immediately right of Military Headquarters (09), on the map’s left side.
- **Usual meeting spot:** The sand lanes beneath the portico, straightening battered practice shields.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/10-Training-Grounds.png|10 — Training Grounds]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

A bent training spear hangs above her bench. She has braided red cord through a cracked bracer. Her injured hand cannot close fully, so she demonstrates grips slowly and without concealment.

## Playing Leandra

Cuts through bravado with practical questions. Praises recovery from mistakes. “Show me how you stand up. Everyone here already knows how to fall.”

**What they want:** Keep a gifted recruit in training after a patron demands his removal for refusing a reckless exercise.

**What they can offer:** A supervised practice session, advice on Akroan military etiquette, and an honest assessment of a mundane weapon. Training grants no automatic feat or combat bonus.

## Secret

She herself once obeyed the same exercise order and permanently injured her hand. She accepted the official story that she had simply lacked discipline.

## A scene ready to play

The patron arrives with an exhibition challenge intended to humiliate the recruit. Leandra asks the party to help propose a fair demonstration or witness what the exercise demands. They can expose the hazard, sponsor a safer test, or persuade the patron to withdraw.

**If left unresolved:** If no one intervenes, she cancels the exercise and faces disciplinary review. The recruit survives but may lose sponsorship.
