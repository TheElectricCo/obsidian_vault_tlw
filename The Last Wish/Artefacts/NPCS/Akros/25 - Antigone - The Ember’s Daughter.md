# Antigone — The Ember’s Daughter

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> New campaign NPC; all personal history and hooks below are new material.

**Role:** Independent smith and lock repairer  
**Identity:** Human woman, 35; a practical devotee of Purphoros.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 25 — the small forge just inside the lower-right wall, between the Veteran’s Rest (23) and the Spear & Shield (24).
- **Usual meeting spot:** The single anvil beneath the soot-blackened street awning.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/25-The-Ember-Anvil.png|25 — The Ember Anvil]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

Her apron is too large at the shoulders, inherited from her mother. One ear bears a tiny bronze hammer. She rests damaged objects in both hands before deciding what can be saved.

## Playing Antigone

Unhurried, blunt, and generous with explanations. “A lock is a promise between a door and its owner. Best discover which one is lying.”

**What they want:** Keep her workshop independent after a military supplier tries to buy her silence about failing fittings.

**What they can offer:** Mundane repairs, custom tools, and assessment of an ordinary lock or key. She can help Plink investigate construction methods, but has no established knowledge of Gronk’s door or a twenty-seventh key.

## Secret

She repaired a disputed batch’s test sample and kept the broken pieces, although the supplier demanded everything be returned. She fears losing contracts more than a personal threat.

## A scene ready to play

The supplier arrives with a generous purchase order conditional on destroying those pieces. Antigone asks the party to take a witnessed copy of her repair notes to an independent reviewer. They may broker a recall, secure alternative work, or arrange a fair hearing.

**If left unresolved:** If ignored, she refuses the order and cuts opening hours. The evidence stays in a locked tool chest until someone requests a formal test.
