# Timarete — The Patient Chisel

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> New campaign NPC; all personal history and hooks below are new material.

**Role:** Stone carver and conservator  
**Identity:** Human woman, 41; a craftswoman who also models faces in clay.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 20 — the workshop lanes at mid-right on the map, below the arena (15) and left of the Golden Lyre (16).
- **Usual meeting spot:** An uphill workshop threshold beside an unfinished relief and trays of drying clay.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/20-Artisan-Quarter.png|20 — Artisan Quarter]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

One eyelid is pale with stone dust. Her hands are broad, her smallest tools delicate. An unfinished face waits beneath a damp cloth that she checks more often than the paying commission.

## Playing Timarete

Listens by turning slightly sideways, as if studying a profile. Rarely praises, never flatters. “A likeness survives the face. Be careful whose lie you ask it to carry.”

**What they want:** Finish a civic commission without carving a deliberately false account of a battle.

**What they can offer:** Conservation advice, impressions of mundane markings by permission, and an expert assessment of later repairs. She will not scrape a sacred or owned object without authority.

## Secret

She has made a faithful small clay model of the rejected version, showing rescuers omitted from the approved design. The model includes details supplied by a still-living witness.

## A scene ready to play

A patron arrives to inspect the relief and demands the model be broken. Timarete asks the party to find a safe witness and a place to keep the model until the payment dispute is heard. They may negotiate a revised scene, buy the model legitimately, or document its evidence.

**If left unresolved:** If ignored, she covers the disputed section and halts work. The patron withholds payment, placing her apprentices’ wages at risk.
