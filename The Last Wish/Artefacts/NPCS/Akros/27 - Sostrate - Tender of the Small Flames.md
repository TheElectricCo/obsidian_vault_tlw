# Sostrate — Tender of the Small Flames

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> New campaign NPC; all personal history and hooks below are new material.

**Role:** Caretaker coordinating the neighbourhood shrines  
**Identity:** Human woman, 59; lay attendant who respects each sanctuary’s own rites.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 27 — the hillside shrines right of the central summit stairs, between the Akropolis (05) and Olive Terraces (28).
- **Usual meeting spot:** The landing between two small shrines, carrying fresh lamp oil up the broad stairs.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/27-Temple-Terraces.png|27 — Temple Terraces]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

Little soot marks darken every finger. Her keys belong to cupboards, not reliquaries. She carries offerings in separate baskets so that one shrine’s gifts never silently become another’s.

## Playing Sostrate

Practical, hospitable, and alert to religious rivalry. “A flame can be shared. The oil still has to be carried uphill.”

**What they want:** Keep the shared meal service running while priests argue over who receives credit for it.

**What they can offer:** Introductions to shrine attendants, public ritual calendars, and explanations of ordinary funerary customs. She does not identify a particular Returned or reveal the stolen divine mask’s location.

## Secret

She borrowed oil from a restricted ceremonial reserve with every intention of replacing it. The reserve is due to be counted before the next delivery.

## A scene ready to play

Two shrine attendants each claim the other has diverted donations. Sostrate asks for help comparing delivery tallies and finding a lawful replacement for the borrowed oil before the dispute ends the meal service. An open accounting or a jointly sponsored donation can resolve it.

**If left unresolved:** If ignored, she admits the borrowing publicly. Meals shrink while the temples debate responsibility, but none loses access to ordinary worship.
