# Menekles — The Last Rivet

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> New campaign NPC; all personal history and hooks below are new material.

**Role:** Garrison armourer and stores custodian  
**Identity:** Human man, 53; meticulous metalworker with poor distance vision.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 12 — the equipment stores below Military Headquarters (09), above the stables (13) on the left side of the map.
- **Usual meeting spot:** A worktable in the vaulted storeroom, under the narrow window.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/12-Armory.png|12 — Armory]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

He leans close enough to a helmet to fog its bronze. Every tool lies in a worn outline on his table. Tiny samples of failed rivets fill an otherwise elegant jewellery box.

## Playing Menekles

Stern about maintenance, unexpectedly tender toward apprentices. “A shield need not be beautiful. It must still be there on the second blow.”

**What they want:** Recall a defective equipment batch before it reaches a public demonstration.

**What they can offer:** Mundane repairs and a factual condition report. Restricted military equipment stays in the stores unless properly released.

## Secret

His apprentice substituted cheaper rivets after Menekles rejected a request for more materials. He wants to save lives without sacrificing the apprentice to a supplier’s defence.

## A scene ready to play

A ceremonial escort is collecting polished shields whose handles have begun loosening. Menekles asks the party to retrieve the issued pieces discreetly and witness a load test. They can expose the supplier, arrange a public recall, or help distribute sound replacement equipment.

**If left unresolved:** Without help, he blocks the remaining issue and risks suspension for delaying the escort. The defective shields remain recoverable from their recipients.
