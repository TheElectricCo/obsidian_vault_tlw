# Melantho — Keeper of the Four Spans

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> New campaign NPC; all personal history and hooks below are new material.

**Role:** Bridge mason and maintenance forewoman  
**Identity:** Human woman, 61; honours Purphoros through her work.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 01 — the bridge entering from the bottom of the map, below the Lower Gate (03).
- **Usual meeting spot:** The cityward end of the bridge, beside a bronze inspection ring. She inspects the outer spans only with an escort and a safety line.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/01-Bridge-of-Pharagax.png|01 — Bridge of Pharagax]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

A small woman in an enormous patched coat kneels with one cheek against the paving. Her chalk has worn a white crescent into her thumbnail. Four knotted cords hang from her belt, one for each span.

## Playing Melantho

Speaks to stone before speaking to people. Precise, dryly affectionate, and furious with anyone who mistakes caution for cowardice. “A crack tells the truth. It is the men paying for the repair who become inventive.”

**What they want:** Close a damaged maintenance anchorage long enough to repair it, without severing the city’s lifeline.

**What they can offer:** A signed inspection sketch, safe maintenance access, and an introduction to Ianthe. She can distinguish workmanship and recent damage, but cannot identify the maker of magical wax.

## Secret

She once signed off an inferior repair to keep supplies moving. She has since paid for replacement metal herself; the receipts could ruin her before they expose the original supplier.

## A scene ready to play

As she tests a bronze ring, its fixing comes away in her hand. She asks for witnesses and an escort beneath the cityward platform before the supplier can replace the evidence. The party can inspect it, seek a closure order, or arrange a guarded temporary repair.

**If left unresolved:** If ignored, she ropes off the anchorage herself and is removed from duty; inspections stop until someone presents her evidence.
