# Brontes — The Measured Flame

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> New campaign NPC; all personal history and hooks below are new material.

**Role:** Foundry master and bronze caster  
**Identity:** Human man, 46; a worshipper of Purphoros with badly scarred forearms.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 14 — the large furnace complex at the far lower left of the city, beside the stables (13).
- **Usual meeting spot:** The safe observation platform overlooking the casting floor; he stops work before inviting visitors closer.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/14-War-Foundry.png|14 — War Foundry]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

His beard is clipped short against sparks. A clay cup of cooled bronze sits on his desk beside a child’s misshapen toy soldier. He moves slowly around heat and quickly around stupidity.

## Playing Brontes

Patient about honest ignorance. His voice drops when angry. “You may hurry the paperwork. The metal keeps its own account.”

**What they want:** Clear a dangerous backlog without accepting adulterated bronze from the cheapest bidder.

**What they can offer:** A maker’s assessment, custom mundane work by agreement, and purchase records for identified batches. Recognising an alloy does not identify who later used it.

## Secret

He accepted one suspect batch on provisional terms to keep wages paid. He quarantined it after testing, but the supplier’s delivery papers falsely show it as fully approved.

## A scene ready to play

A wagon arrives to collect castings under that false approval. Brontes asks for witnesses to a comparison test and someone to protect the sample during review. The party can follow the paperwork, confront the supplier, or negotiate replacement metal while workers remain paid.

**If left unresolved:** If ignored, he closes the affected furnace. Workers lose shifts while the supplier claims the delay proves incompetence.
