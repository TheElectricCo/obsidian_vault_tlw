# Ianthe — The Rider Who Counts Twice

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> Ianthe is established in [[001 - Phanarax Brug#Patrouillecommandant Ianthe]]. Her protective manner and command of four pegasus riders come from that adventure; age, personal history, and this local hook are new. The rescue is conditional on what actually happens at the table.

**Role:** Akroan pegasus patrol commander  
**Identity:** Human woman, 36; devoted to Iroas and the people under her protection.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 02 — the colossal guardian immediately inside the Lower Gate (03), at the bottom centre of the city.
- **Usual meeting spot:** The watch post at the colossus’s feet; on patrol when the bridge alarms sound.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/02-Colossus-of-Akros.png|02 — Colossus of Akros]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

Her red cloak is neatly mended, her bronze helmet dented above one ear. Before she answers a difficult question, she counts the people around her. She counts again before leaving.

## Playing Ianthe

Brisk with rank, gentle with frightened witnesses. She asks about injuries before weapons. “Names after water. Everyone breathes first.”

**What they want:** Keep her patrol ready while securing an honest account of the bridge incident, if it has occurred.

**What they can offer:** A reliable introduction to the watch, water and first aid, and help finding the temporary accommodation promised in the bridge adventure. The field flasks in that adventure remain limited supplies.

## Secret

She has quietly postponed a readiness inspection because one rider is recovering from panic after a fall. She needs a replacement observer, not someone to shame the rider.

## A scene ready to play

A superior requests an immediate demonstration flight while an urgent inspection of the bridge is pending. Ianthe asks the party to accompany a ground inspection or carry a truthful report explaining the delay. They can support her, offer another plan, or decline without losing her basic protection.

**If left unresolved:** If the report is delayed, her unit is assigned ceremonial duty; rescue coverage becomes thinner until the readiness dispute is resolved.
