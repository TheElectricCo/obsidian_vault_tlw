# Kallias — The Margin Keeper

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> New campaign NPC; all personal history and hooks below are new material.

**Role:** Deputy civic archivist  
**Identity:** Human man, 56; an archivist with a remarkable memory for errors.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 22 in the corrected scenery numbering — Archive of Deeds. The printed legend repeats 21 for this entry, and no distinct 22 marker is clearly visible on the map.
- **Usual meeting spot:** For play, place its records hall beside Town Hall (17), reached from the civic forecourt. This precise placement is proposed, not a verified printed-map position. Kallias sits at the first long desk beneath the clerestory.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/22-Archive-of-Deeds.png|22 — Archive of Deeds]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

His robe has a polished patch where his elbow rests on the desk. A narrow strip of parchment marks his place in every conversation; he writes down promises before replying.

## Playing Kallias

Speaks gently and insists on distinctions between originals, copies, and hearsay. “An absent name is not evidence of an absent life. Someone may simply have turned the page.”

**What they want:** Preserve an original register before a politically convenient replacement becomes the only accessible record.

**What they can offer:** Access to public deeds, certified searches, and supervised comparisons. Searches for Arlen, Gronk, or a relic can begin here, but no relevant entry is established by this note.

## Secret

He hid a misfiled original casualty roll in the repair queue after recognising an unauthorised alteration. Its official absence is now being used to discredit another petitioner.

## A scene ready to play

A supervisor orders the damaged rolls pulped after copying. Kallias asks the party to obtain a preservation order and witness a comparison before the cart leaves. They can show the discrepancy, pay for lawful conservation, or persuade the supervisor to retain the originals.

**If left unresolved:** If ignored, he delays the cart by reporting a pest concern. That buys a day and attracts inspection of his own conduct.
