# Myrrine — The Second Edge

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> New campaign NPC; all personal history and hooks below are new material.

**Role:** Second-hand arms dealer and equipment appraiser  
**Identity:** Human woman, 37; former caravan guard.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 19 — the equipment market right of Town Hall (17), below the central residential lanes.
- **Usual meeting spot:** A narrow stall hung with used shields beneath a burgundy awning.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/19-Warriors-Market.png|19 — Warriors Market]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

An old scar crosses the bridge of her nose. She ties a scrap of blue wool to every repaired item and can tell a blade’s balance before looking at its decoration.

## Playing Myrrine

Quick humour, slow trust. Invites customers to inspect the flaws first. “If I wished to rob you, I would polish it and call it a hero’s sword.”

**What they want:** Keep her business independent of a supplier offering cheap arms with troublesome ownership histories.

**What they can offer:** Honest mundane appraisals, repair referrals, and trade for useful equipment. She identifies wear and maker’s marks, not magical properties by sight.

## Secret

She bought a small lot of military surplus whose release tablet may be forged. She has held the unsold pieces aside but already sold one shield to a young guard.

## A scene ready to play

A buyer returns with a shield seized as stolen property. Myrrine asks the party to trace the release document and recover the guard’s purchase before the seller vanishes from the market. They can negotiate restitution, seek a civic review, or follow the supplier’s local warehouse trail.

**If left unresolved:** If ignored, she reports the lot herself and closes temporarily. A warehouse receipt remains with the impounded goods.
