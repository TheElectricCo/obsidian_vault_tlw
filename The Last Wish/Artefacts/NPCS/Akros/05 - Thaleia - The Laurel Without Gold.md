# Thaleia — The Laurel Without Gold

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> New campaign NPC; all personal history and hooks below are new material.

**Role:** Priestess responsible for petitions and sanctuary supplicants  
**Identity:** Human woman, 55; former athlete and priestess of Iroas.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 05 — the summit sanctuary at the very top centre of the map, beyond the Upper Gate (04).
- **Usual meeting spot:** The temple’s outer steps after morning offerings; she receives private petitions in a side courtyard.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/05-Akropolis-of-Iroas.png|05 — Akropolis of Iroas]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

A wreath of ordinary olive leaves rests on close-cropped silver hair. Her ceremonial robe cannot hide the old stiffness in her throwing shoulder. She carries petition tablets herself.

## Playing Thaleia

Warm until someone glorifies needless suffering. Asks who carried the wounded home. “Tell me what your victory cost the people who never entered the contest.”

**What they want:** Keep public worship tied to courage and mercy while donors press for a triumphal spectacle.

**What they can offer:** Sanctuary, a patient hearing, introductions among the shrines, and access to ordinary temple records. Divine favour and miraculous healing are not automatic rewards.

## Secret

She has delayed endorsing an influential champion because she suspects he abandoned a companion. She has testimony, but the witness fears losing a military pension.

## A scene ready to play

A donor threatens to withdraw funding for communal meals unless his champion receives a blessing before witnesses. Thaleia asks the party to protect the witness’s livelihood while checking the account. A public hearing, private restitution, or withdrawal of the champion’s claim could all avert the bargain.

**If left unresolved:** If nobody helps, she refuses the endorsement and the meal fund contracts. She continues worship and petitions openly for replacement donations.
