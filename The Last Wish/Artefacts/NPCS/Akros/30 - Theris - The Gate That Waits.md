# Theris — The Gate That Waits

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> New NPC. The scenery shows an East Gate opening toward a mountain track; [[1ste versie overkoepelend verhaal]] establishes Akros’s isolation and the bridge as its only approach. The track is therefore not a usable bypass during the present crisis. The exact obstruction remains for the DM to decide.

**Role:** Commander of the secondary gate watch  
**Identity:** Human woman, 43; a cautious officer accustomed to caravan traffic.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 30 — the secondary gateway in the lower-right city wall, below the Spear & Shield (24).
- **Usual meeting spot:** The cityward inspection bench inside the gateway; no one is routinely waved out onto the mountain road.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/30-East-Gate.png|30 — East Gate]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

A red cord binds her greying hair beneath the helmet. Her cloak smells of cold stone. She has nailed an empty route board above the desk and refuses to fill it with optimistic guesses.

## Playing Theris

Calm, unsentimental, protective of desperate travellers. “A road drawn on a map is not a road you can walk today.”

**What they want:** Keep residents from mistaking the visible mountain track for a safe escape from the city’s isolation.

**What they can offer:** Honest gate reports, access to the protected inner inspection area, and a supervised look beyond the threshold when conditions allow. She cannot provide a working alternate exit.

## Secret

A trader has offered to finance a patrol in exchange for exclusive control of any recovered route. Theris has accepted provisions for a survey but has not promised access or exclusivity.

## A scene ready to play

A stranded merchant is selling passage on a route he has never tested under the present conditions. Theris asks the party to question him before frightened households pay. Checking his evidence, refunding customers, or surveying only the defensible near approach can expose what is actually known.

**If left unresolved:** If ignored, she suspends outward passage and the merchant accuses her of protecting a monopoly. A public review can still establish the facts.
