# Rhode — The Quiet Horizon

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> New campaign NPC; all personal history and hooks below are new material.

**Role:** Keeper of the cliffside tavern  
**Identity:** Human woman, 52; former mountain messenger.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 26 — the cliff-edge tavern at the far lower right of the city, above the outer wall and right of the residential lanes.
- **Usual meeting spot:** The open drinking terrace beneath the loggia at sunset; she checks its parapet every morning.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/26-Cliffwatch.png|26 — Cliffwatch]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

A grey braid hangs over a coat faded almost white. She wears sturdy boots even while serving. Her cups have broad bases suited to sudden gusts.

## Playing Rhode

Comfortable with silence. Asks before sitting beside a solitary guest. “The view is free. The wine is not. The silence is yours to keep.”

**What they want:** Protect a peaceful meeting place from a speculative project that would close the terrace to ordinary residents.

**What they can offer:** A quiet meeting space, reliable observations of activity visible from the terrace, and knowledge of old messenger routes. No cliff route she knows bypasses the campaign’s severed approach to Akros.

## Secret

She kept a private record of cracks in the retaining wall while delaying a formal report, afraid the tavern would be condemned before she could finance repairs.

## A scene ready to play

A visitor notices a fresh crack just as a wealthy buyer offers immediate cash for the property. Rhode asks for an independent safety inspection and help organising temporary repairs. The party can support a cooperative purchase, negotiate funding, or expose a misleading survey.

**If left unresolved:** If ignored, she closes the exposed terrace herself and serves indoors. The buyer spreads rumours that the entire building is failing.
