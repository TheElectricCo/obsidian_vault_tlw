# Eryx — The Unfinished Toast

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> New campaign NPC; all personal history and hooks below are new material.

**Role:** Innkeeper and retired shield-bearer  
**Identity:** Human man, 67; a veteran with an old leg injury.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 23 — the tavern near the lower-right wall, left of the Ember Anvil (25). The printed legend calls the Veteran’s Rest 22; use scenery number 23 and the visible map marker 23.
- **Usual meeting spot:** The hearth-side table beneath the faded banner; he serves food before asking anyone to tell a war story.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/23-The-Veterans-Rest.png|23 — The Veteran’s Rest]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

A broad man pared thin by age, he wears a wool wrap around one knee. He polishes a dented cup that he never fills. The chairs nearest the hearth are always left easy to reach.

## Playing Eryx

Dry wit and long pauses. Never presses a guest to describe an injury. “The dead have had enough speeches. Pass the bread to the living.”

**What they want:** Keep his inn a refuge rather than a recruiting ground or a stage for borrowed heroism.

**What they can offer:** Beds and meals by agreement, introductions to willing veterans, and possible accommodation if Ianthe arranges it. He never supplies a guest list to strangers.

## Secret

He carries survivor’s guilt over a retreat he once described as a glorious stand. Another veteran has arrived carrying the order that proves who really stayed behind.

## A scene ready to play

A celebrated guest demands an evening of public battle stories, unaware that the surviving witness is in the room. Eryx asks the party to help arrange a private meeting first. They can support a correction, negotiate a memorial, or protect the witness from being turned into entertainment.

**If left unresolved:** If the story is told unchallenged, the witness leaves for Cliffwatch rather than vanishing. Eryx follows later to offer an apology.
