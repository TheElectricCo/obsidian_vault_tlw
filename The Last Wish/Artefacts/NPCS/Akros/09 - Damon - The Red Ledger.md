# Damon — The Red Ledger

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> New campaign NPC; all personal history and hooks below are new material.

**Role:** Military logistics officer  
**Identity:** Human man, 45; a career administrator who has served in the field.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 09 — the military command courtyard on the left side of the map, below the barracks (11) and beside the training grounds (10).
- **Usual meeting spot:** The shaded courtyard strategy table, surrounded by supply tablets instead of battle maps.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/09-Military-Headquarters.png|09 — Military Headquarters]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

His cuirass is serviceable rather than splendid. An old campaign knife holds down a stack of requisitions. His fingers bear as much ink as any civilian clerk’s.

## Playing Damon

Counts practical consequences aloud. Gives uncomfortable news without ornament. “Six hundred spears are six hundred promises that someone will eat tomorrow.”

**What they want:** Keep training, patrols, and provisioning functioning while investigating shortages without starting a panic.

**What they can offer:** Limited official work, an introduction to the relevant stores officer, and supply records connected to an accepted investigation. He cannot assign the whole garrison to a private search.

## Secret

He temporarily moved grain reserved for a ceremonial feast into ordinary soldiers’ rations. The food was needed, but he lacks authority for the transfer.

## A scene ready to play

Two signed requisitions claim the same wagon of grain. Damon wants independent witnesses at its unloading because he suspects a duplicate order. The party can trace the seals, accompany the wagon, or help negotiate an honest redistribution before an innocent quartermaster is accused.

**If left unresolved:** Without a resolution, ceremonial purchases are frozen and ration queues lengthen; Damon records his transfer and accepts the inquiry.
