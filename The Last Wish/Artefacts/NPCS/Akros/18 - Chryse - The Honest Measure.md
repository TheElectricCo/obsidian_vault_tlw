# Chryse — The Honest Measure

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> New campaign NPC; all personal history and hooks below are new material.

**Role:** Grain factor and elected stall representative  
**Identity:** Human woman, 49; trader and household provider.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 18 — the main market left of Town Hall (17), below the Hall of Champions (08).
- **Usual meeting spot:** A grain stall beneath an ochre awning near the central fountain.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/18-Agora---Main-Market.png|18 — Agora - Main Market]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

She wears bronze weights on a cord like a necklace. Her sleeves smell of flour and orange peel. She tests a measure with her palm before setting it on a scale.

## Playing Chryse

Expansive in greeting, exact in bargains. Gives away bruised fruit without ceremony. “A fair measure will not make me rich today. It may keep me here next year.”

**What they want:** Keep staple food affordable without bankrupting the traders who bring it into the city.

**What they can offer:** Fair prices, a meal, and knowledge of current supply arrangements. She can coordinate merchants’ voluntary cooperation, not command their stock.

## Secret

She is concealing the size of her own remaining stock to prevent a rush. She has enough to bridge a short interruption, but not enough to fulfil every promise she has made.

## A scene ready to play

Two neighbours accuse each other of short measures while a crowd gathers at her stall. Chryse suspects a tampered communal weight. She asks the party to compare it with an independent standard before rumour becomes a riot. Investigation, calm testimony, and an agreed temporary ration can all help.

**If left unresolved:** If the dispute spreads, several stalls close early. Prices rise locally until someone demonstrates which weight was altered.
