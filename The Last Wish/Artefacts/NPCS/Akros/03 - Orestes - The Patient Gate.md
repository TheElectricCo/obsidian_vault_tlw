# Orestes — The Patient Gate

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> New campaign NPC; all personal history and hooks below are new material.

**Role:** Gate sergeant and arrivals clerk  
**Identity:** Human man, 48; former infantryman with a damaged knee.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 03 — the outer gate between the bridge (01) and the colossus (02), at the bottom centre of the map.
- **Usual meeting spot:** A little arrivals desk inside the gatehouse, within sight of the braziers.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/03-Lower-Gate.png|03 — Lower Gate]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

His spear stands within reach, but his hands usually hold a cup and a blunt stylus. A wooden footrest keeps one knee straight beneath the desk.

## Playing Orestes

Lets silence do the work of interrogation. Remembers luggage better than faces. “Tell me what you brought in. Then tell me what you meant to bring.”

**What they want:** Let legitimate travellers through without becoming the officer blamed for every missing crate in Akros.

**What they can offer:** A witnessed account of an arrival, directions, and a short grace period for missing paperwork. His ledger records what was declared, not what was truly inside a sealed crate.

## Secret

He admitted a delivery against an incomplete receipt because its driver was collapsing from exhaustion. The driver is safe; the crate’s destination was later altered in another hand.

## A scene ready to play

A porter’s innocent mule is about to be confiscated over that altered receipt. Orestes will show the original entry if the party agrees to trace the handoff before the duty officer arrives. Interviewing the driver, finding the recipient, or proving the forged addition can settle the matter.

**If left unresolved:** Without intervention, the mule is impounded and the porter loses a day’s wages; the suspect delivery record remains available for later investigation.
