# Asterion — The Unbowed

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> New campaign NPC; all personal history and hooks below are new material.

**Role:** Commander of the summit checkpoint  
**Identity:** Minotaur man, 42; an exacting worshipper of Iroas.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 04 — the high central stair gate, directly below the Akropolis (05) and above the monolith (06).
- **Usual meeting spot:** The shaded uphill checkpoint, where he inspects permits beside the bronze doors.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/04-Upper-Gate.png|04 — Upper Gate]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

One horn has a bronze cap; both carry tiny devotional knots. He lowers his head to hear children and remains perfectly upright before magistrates.

## Playing Asterion

Soft-spoken and literal. Extends courtesies even while refusing entry. “A name may open a ledger. It does not open this gate.”

**What they want:** Apply the same summit access rules to wealthy patrons, soldiers, and ordinary petitioners.

**What they can offer:** An escorted appointment upstairs and a precise explanation of local access rules. He cannot grant unrestricted entry to the summit.

## Secret

His younger brother forged an elite household’s permit to deliver food to unpaid servants. Asterion has kept the permit as evidence but has not yet submitted it.

## A scene ready to play

A richly dressed patron presents a genuine permit for an unlisted armed entourage while a servant carrying medicine has none. Asterion asks for witnesses to a recorded exception rather than a bribe. The party can help verify the medicine, challenge the entourage’s entry, or fetch the official empowered to amend the permits.

**If left unresolved:** If publicly humiliated, he becomes formal rather than violent. The permit dispute moves to the Town Hall, where it can still be resolved.
