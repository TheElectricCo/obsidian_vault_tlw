# Phaedra — The Unbridled

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> New campaign NPC; all personal history and hooks below are new material.

**Role:** Military stablekeeper and pegasus ground attendant  
**Identity:** Centaur woman, 31; a patient expert in equine behaviour.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 13 — the stable yards in the lower-left military quarter, between the armory (12) and the outer wall.
- **Usual meeting spot:** The ordinary warhorse courtyard by day; the patrol’s separate open landing yard when riders return. The landing yard is a proposed addition, not a separate map marker.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/13-Stables.png|13 — Stables]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

Her dark flank is dusted with pale straw. A leather apron holds brushes and soft cloths. She lets a nervous horse smell her hand before touching its tack.

## Playing Phaedra

Plainspoken, observant, and dismissive of prestige in a saddle. “He heard the buckle before he saw your hands. Try again, slower.”

**What they want:** Prevent an exhausted patrol mount from being sent out merely to satisfy a timetable.

**What they can offer:** Care for ordinary mounts, advice on tack, and a respectful introduction to patrol riders. Pegasi are willing allies, not stock she can sell or assign.

## Secret

She has removed a damaged harness from service without logging who last used it; she suspects a hurried repair, but fears an accusation will hide the real failure.

## A scene ready to play

A rider insists a reluctant pegasus is simply disobedient. Phaedra asks for help examining its harness and reconstructing the last landing. Patient observation, testimony, and a replacement harness can solve the dispute; forcing the creature only deepens its distress.

**If left unresolved:** If ignored, she refuses to fit the unsafe harness and the departure is delayed. The damaged strap remains evidence.
