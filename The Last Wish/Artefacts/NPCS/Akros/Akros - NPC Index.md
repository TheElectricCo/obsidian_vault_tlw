# Akros — NPC Index

Thirty contacts, one for each point of interest in [[Akros - Scenery Gallery]]. Each has a home location, a recognisable manner, something to offer, a private vulnerability, and an encounter with several possible resolutions. The relationships connect the city without requiring every local trouble to have the same mastermind.

> [!warning] DM reference
> These notes contain secrets. Ianthe, Theron Kalies, and Lysandra Kyros build on existing campaign references; their notes identify the established details. The other contacts and all new local disputes are campaign additions. Encounter prompts describe situations to introduce, not events that have already happened.

## Threads to pick up during play

- **Unsafe metal and disputed deliveries:** Melantho (01), Menekles (12), Brontes (14), Myrrine (19), and Antigone (25) hold different pieces of a supply problem. Their documents and samples establish a useful trail; who is responsible remains open. A defective rivet does not prove who placed the bridge’s black seal.
- **Whose victory is remembered:** Thaleia (05), Nikomache (06), Philomelos (08), Lysandra (16), Timarete (20), Kallias (22), and Eryx (23) create a trail through public honour, missing names, and living witnesses. Link their historical incidents together only if useful; they need not all concern the same battle.
- **The price of keeping a city alive:** Damon (09), Nerissa (11), Chryse (18), Maia (21), Sostrate (27), and Ione (28) show the effects of shortages on ordinary lives. Resolving the supply dispute can ease several problems without resolving the divine crisis.

## Established party leads

- **Arowel:** Lysandra (16) honours the existing invitation. Songs and artists can open research opportunities; no relic’s location is decided here.
- **Darius:** Theron (07) is the established auction contact. Timarete (20) and Kallias (22) provide possible independent checks. Lot XVII’s authenticity and the identity of the intruder remain open.
- **Dorian:** The archive (22), consenting local witnesses, and public records offer places to begin inquiries. No sighting of Arlen or discovery of a Bewaarders relic is established by these NPCs.
- **Plink:** Antigone (25) offers practical expertise with locks, and Timon (29) understands local deliveries. Neither already knows Gronk’s whereabouts or possesses the twenty-seventh key.

## Continuity at the table

- The bridge encounter does not identify the author of the black seal. These NPCs can examine evidence without retrospectively choosing its culprit.
- The overarching plot draft makes the bridge the only approach during Akros’s isolation. The East Gate’s depicted mountain road provides no working bypass; Theris’s note preserves this restriction.
- Macar remains [[MACAR - King of Akros|king]], and [[CYMEDE - Chancellor|Cymede]] remains chancellor. New clerks, caretakers, and officers do not replace them.
- Secrets describe facts about these new local stories; a character’s suspicions remain suspicions. Use ordinary 2014 rules and any Farore homebrew for checks, costs, and services. No new combat statistics, spells, automatic boons, or player-character dialogue are prescribed.
