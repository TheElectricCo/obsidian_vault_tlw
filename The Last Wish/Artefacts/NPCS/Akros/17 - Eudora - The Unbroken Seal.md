# Eudora — The Unbroken Seal

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> New campaign NPC; all personal history and hooks below are new material.

**Role:** Deputy petitions clerk  
**Identity:** Human woman, 34; civic official serving under the existing administration.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 17 — the civic building at the centre-lower part of the map, below the monolith (06) and between the markets (18–19).
- **Usual meeting spot:** The portico’s petition desk in the morning; a side office overlooking the forecourt fountain after noon.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/17-Town-Hall.png|17 — Town Hall]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

Her cuffs are darned with thread exactly matching the original cloth. A chipped sealing stamp hangs at her waist, wrapped to prevent accidental impressions. She reads every petition to its end.

## Playing Eudora

Patient, wry, and quietly stubborn. Translates official language into ordinary speech. “A missing form can be replaced. A missing person requires us to stand up.”

**What they want:** Keep access to civic hearings from becoming something only patrons can purchase.

**What they can offer:** Petition guidance, a certified copy of a relevant public order, and appointments she is authorised to arrange. She is neither chancellor nor a substitute for [[CYMEDE - Chancellor|Cymede]].

## Secret

She has been giving vulnerable applicants cancelled appointment slots without approval. A senior clerk is collecting the altered schedules as evidence against her.

## A scene ready to play

A widow’s compensation hearing is displaced by a merchant’s boundary petition. Eudora asks the party to help establish the actual order of filings before the clerk closes the docket. They can find witnesses, secure the merchant’s cooperation, or carry an appeal through proper channels.

**If left unresolved:** If exposed without support, she is transferred to copying duties. Her annotated schedule can still establish which applications were displaced.
