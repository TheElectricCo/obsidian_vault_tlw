# Timon — The Borrowed Staff

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> New campaign NPC; all personal history and hooks below are new material.

**Role:** Guide for pilgrims and informal letter carrier  
**Identity:** Human man, 29; walker with a limp and a gift for remembering faces.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 29 — the stepped processional road in the middle-right city, between the civic centre and the residential lanes (21).
- **Usual meeting spot:** The round wayside shrine at the bend; he arranges short uphill stages for elderly pilgrims.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/29-The-Pilgrim-Road.png|29 — The Pilgrim Road]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

His staff is polished where many other hands have borrowed it. A little bell on his satchel announces his arrival before his uneven tread does. He carries letters in a waxed cloth roll.

## Playing Timon

Cheerful without forcing cheer on others. Offers an arm rather than taking one. “There is no prize for reaching the shrine too tired to remember why you came.”

**What they want:** Carry out his small duties without letting a well-paying client turn him into an unwitting spy.

**What they can offer:** Local guidance, consensual message delivery, and introductions along the road. Similar handwriting or a familiar description never proves a letter came from Arlen or Gronk.

## Secret

He accepted payment to note who collected a particular sealed letter. He has not delivered that report, and now recognises how easily his observations could endanger someone.

## A scene ready to play

A stranger offers the party money to follow Timon while he assists pilgrims. Timon, if approached honestly, admits the earlier commission and asks for help returning the fee and protecting the recipient’s privacy. The party can investigate the client, mediate, or arrange witnessed delivery.

**If left unresolved:** If ignored, he returns the fee himself and warns the recipient. The client seeks a less scrupulous observer, leaving another local lead.
