# Ione — Root and Stone

> [!warning] DM only — continuity
> New campaign NPC; all personal history and hooks below are new material.

**Role:** Olive grower and irrigation steward  
**Identity:** Human woman, 46; tenant farmer and organiser of seasonal labour.

## Position on the map

- **Map point:** 28 — the rows of olive trees inside the upper-right walls, above the arena (15) and right of the Temple Terraces (27).
- **Usual meeting spot:** A low irrigation sluice beneath the oldest olive tree; she works among the baskets during harvest.
- **Map:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Maps/Akros.png|Akros city map]]
- **Scenery:** [[The Last Wish/Artefacts/Scenery/Akros/28-Olive-Terraces.png|28 — Olive Terraces]]

Directions refer to the displayed map, with its title upright. This is the NPC’s usual location, not a guarantee they are always present.

## First impression

Silver leaves cling to a sun-faded headscarf. Her knife is kept for grafting, not threats. A smooth olive pit turns between her fingers when she calculates a debt.

## Playing Ione

Wry, patient, and difficult to hurry. Notices labour that others overlook. “A tree remembers who watered it. Landlords have a shorter memory.”

**What they want:** Protect the workers’ water allocation and enough oil for neighbourhood kitchens before prestige orders consume the harvest.

**What they can offer:** Food, fair seasonal work, knowledge of upper water channels, and introductions to growers. She has no hidden route through the divine isolation around Akros.

## Secret

She has been diverting a small part of her own allotment to an ailing neighbour. The books make the neighbour appear more productive, exposing both to a rent increase.

## A scene ready to play

A new lease map places the communal sluice inside a private holding. Ione asks the party to witness the old channel’s course and find the previous deed. They can establish the boundary, negotiate shared water, or secure a temporary arrangement before the next irrigation turn.

**If left unresolved:** Without a settlement, irrigation is delayed and she prioritises drinking water. The trees survive a short delay, but yields and wages suffer.
